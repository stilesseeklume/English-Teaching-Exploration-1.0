# 第6讲 介词辨析：其他

## 概述

*本讲辨析其他常见的介词用法，包括穿衣介词、in one's mind 与 on one's mind、beside/besides/except/except for、apart from、on one's own 与 of one's own、in turn 与 in return、other than 与 rather than、表示工具手段的介词、表原因理由的介词、以及一些重要的 be + adj. + prep. 搭配。*

## 详细内容

### 考点1. 表示穿衣 over, in 与 on

- 人在衣服里面用 **in**
- 衣服穿在人身上用 **on**
- 某衣服在另一衣服的外面用 **over**

He put his coat over the girl lest she catch cold. 他把大衣披在女孩身上唯恐她着凉。

### 考点2. in one's mind 与 on one's mind

- **in one's mind** 表示"在心里，在想"
- **on one's mind** 表示"压在心头"

例句：
- Do you have anyone in particular in your mind? 你心中有没有确定的人选？
- You look worried. What's on your mind? 你看起来很忧虑。怎么了？

### 考点3. beside, besides, except, except for, except that

#### A. beside 旁边
There is an egg beside the box. 在盒子的旁边有一个鸡蛋。

#### B. besides "除了……还有"，表示的是**追加**关系
Five other representatives arrived late besides me. 除了我**还有**五位代表来晚了。

#### C. except "除了……"，表示的是**剔除**的关系
All the other five representatives arrived late except me. 除了我，其他的五位代表都来晚了。

#### D. except for 在整体上给以定论后，再从局部上加以修正
与 except 不同：except 所"排除"的部分与前面词所表示的整体属于**同类**，是部分与整体的关系；而 except for 所"排除"的部分与前面所述的整体词往往**不是同类**。
- Your essay is well written except for one or two minor grammatical mistakes. 你的作文除了一两处小的语法错误外，写得很好。

> 若用于句首，则 except for 与 except 同义（但 except 通常不用于句首）。
> - Except for me, everyone passed the exam. 除了我之外，大家都通过了考试。

#### E. except that 后面跟句子
She is quite attractive except that she is a little short. 除去稍微有点矮之外，她看起来相当吸引人。

### 考点4. apart from 与 aside from

apart from 可以表示 besides, except, 和 except for 三种含义，需要根据上下文判断：
- Apart from English, he has a good command of Russian and French. 除英语外，他还精通俄语和法语。（= besides）
- He has no interests, apart from his work. 他除了自己的工作外，没什么兴趣爱好。（= except）
- It's a good paper, apart from a few spelling mistakes. 这是一篇好论文，只是有几处拼写错误。（= except for）

### 考点5. on one's own 与 of one's own

- **on one's own** 独自，不依靠别人
- **of one's own** 属于某人自己的

例句：
- He finished his homework on his own. 他独自完成作业。
- He has a car of his own. 他有一辆属于自己的车。

### 考点6. in turn 与 in return

- **in turn** 依次；反过来
- **in return** 作为回报

### 考点7. other than 与 rather than

#### A. rather than 是连词，前后两端所连的词性是一致的
- He was busy writing a letter rather than reading the newspaper. 他那时是在写信而不是看报纸。
- These shoes are comfortable rather than pretty. 这些鞋子谈不上漂亮，但穿起来倒很舒服。

#### B. other than "除了"，相当于 except
- He has visited every country in Asia other than Japan. 除了日本之外，他访问了所有的亚洲国家。
- There's nobody here other than me. 除了我这里没别人。

### 考点8. 表示工具或手段的介词 by, with

#### A. by 用某种方式
- We went in by (=through) the front door. 我们是通过前门进去的。
- Do you wish to be paid in cash or by cheque? 你是希望用现金支付还是支票支付？

#### B. by 后面也常用动名词表示"以何种方式"
He learned English by listening to the radio. 他通过听收音机来学英语。

#### C. with 表示"用某种有形的工具"
- write with a pen 用钢笔写
- see with naked eyes 用肉眼看
- strike with a hammer 用锤子敲打
- He broke the window with a stone. 他用石头把玻璃砸坏了。

### 考点9. 表原因或理由的介词：for, at, with

#### A. for 表示原因，常与 sorry, famous, punish, praise, thank, blame 等词连用
I am sorry for what I said to you. 我后悔不该对你讲那些话。

#### B. at 指情感变化的原因，意为"因听到或看到而……"
- He was surprised at the news. 听到这消息他大吃一惊。
- He got angry at what I said. 他听到我的话后生气了。
- be amazed at / be annoyed at / be astonished at / be frustrated at

#### C. with 指生理上或情感上的原因引起行为或外表变化
- Hearing the news, he jumped with joy. 听到这个消息，他欣喜若狂。
- He was shaking with anger. 他气得浑身发抖。

#### D. out of 表示动机的起因，常译为"出于……"
He asked the question out of curiosity. 他出于好奇才问了那个问题。

#### E. through 多表示因局部而影响全局的原因
The war was lost through bad organization. 战争因组织不周而失败了。

### 考点10. be tired of 与 be tired from

- **be tired of** "讨厌，厌烦"（类似：be sick of, be fed up with）
- **be tired from** "因为……而劳累"

例句：
- I'm tired of having to pretend all the time. 我讨厌老得装假。
- You must be tired from the long flight. Please take a rest today. 长时间地搭乘飞机您一定累了，今天就请好好休息吧。

### 考点11. be famous for, be famous to 与 be famous as

类似的还有 be known to/as/for。
- **be famous to sb.** 对于某人来说是出名的
- **be famous for sth.** 因为某事而出名
- **be famous as...** 作为……而出名

例句：
- He is famous for his generosity to the poor. 他因对穷人慷慨相助而出名。
- In fact, London is famous as a classical cultural city. 实际上，伦敦是以文化古城而著名。

### 考点12. be strict with 与 be strict in

- **be strict with sb.** 对某人要求严格
- **be strict in sth.** 在……方面严格要求

例句：
- While he loves his students, he is very strict with them. 虽然他爱他的学生，可是他对他们很严格。
- He was sent away because he was not strict in his work. 因对工作要求不严格，他被开除了。

### 考点13. be familiar with 与 be familiar to

- **sb. is familiar with sth.** 某人熟悉某事物
- **sth. is familiar to sb.** 某事物对于某人来说是熟悉的

例句：
- He is familiar with export procedure. 他熟悉出口手续。
- Your name is familiar to me. 你的名字我是熟悉的。

### 考点14. in the light of 与 by the light of

#### A. in the light of 鉴于，考虑到
（light 是"光"的意思，有光就能看到事物。链接：keep sb. in the dark 使某人蒙在鼓里。）
- In the light of these changes, we must revise our plan. 鉴于这些变化，我们必须重新修订我们的计划。
- In the light of his remarks, we rejected her offer. 鉴于他的评语，我们拒绝了她的提议。

#### B. by the light of 借着……的光线
She was reading by the light of a candle. 她在蜡烛光下读书。

### 考点15. despite, in spite of, regardless of

#### A. despite 比 in spite of 严谨一些，但意思没有区别
都可译为"虽然"或"尽管"，是介词，后面都接名词性成分。
- The plane came down safely in spite of the mist. 尽管有雾，飞机还是安全着陆了。
- Despite the great efforts, we failed to carry our plans through. 尽管我们努力了，但还是不能完成我们的计划。

> 后面接句子一般用 despite/in spite of **the fact that** 的结构。
> English became the official language in spite of/despite the fact that the population is largely Chinese. 尽管人口主要是华人，英语却成为官方语言。

#### B. regardless of 一般用于客观上无法避免的事实
I shall go regardless of the weather. 无论天气好坏我都去。

### 考点16. owing to, due to, because of, thanks to, on account of, as a result of

这几个短语后面常跟名词性成分，都有"由于，因为"之意。

| 短语 | 作状语 | 作表语 | 句首 |
|------|:------:|:------:|:----:|
| because of | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ |
| thanks to | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ |
| on account of | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ |
| as a result of | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ |
| owing to | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| due to | ✓ | ✓ | ✗（一般不用于句首） |

例句：
- The flight was postponed on account of bad weather. 因为天气恶劣，飞行延期了。
- Because of my bad leg, I couldn't walk so fast as the others. 由于我的腿不好，我不能像其他人走那么快。
- As a result of the flood, thousands of people lost their homes. 因为发大水，成千上万的人失去了家园。
- I was well again, thanks to the doctor. 由于那位医生，我又恢复了健康。
- He was out of work owing to a physical injury. 他因为受伤而失业了。
- Joe's application to the university was not accepted due to his failing English. 乔因英语不及格，申请那所大学没被录取。

### 考点17. like 可以作介词也可以作动词

#### A. like 作"像"讲时是介词，作"喜欢"讲时是动词
- To argue with him is like beating the air. 和他辩论是白费力气。
- He likes his tea neither too hot nor too cold. 他想要杯不凉不热的茶。

#### B. What be...like 常询问事物的特点
询问人时多是问人的外貌、成就、品质、性格等内在的东西。
- What's the weather like today? 今天天气怎么样？
- What's your sister like? 你姐姐怎么样？ — She is kind. 她很善良。
- What does your sister look like? 你姐姐长什么样子？
