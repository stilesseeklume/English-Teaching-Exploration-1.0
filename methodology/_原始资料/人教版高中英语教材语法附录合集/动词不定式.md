# 动词不定式

## 概述
动词不定式是英语非谓语动词的一种，形式上由"to + 动词原形"构成（有时省略to）。不定式保留着动词的部分特性，可以带宾语和状语构成不定式短语，有时态和语态的变化。在句法功能上，动词不定式（短语）与名词、形容词或副词作用类似，在句子中可以充当主语、表语、宾语、定语、状语、宾语补足语等几乎所有句子成分。

## 详细内容

### 1. 动词不定式的基本形式
- **基本形式**：to + 动词原形（有时省略to）
- **否定形式**：not to do
- **时态变化**：一般时(to do)、进行时(to be doing)、完成时(to have done)
- **语态变化**：主动语态(to do)、被动语态(to be done)

### 2. 动词不定式的句法功能

#### 2.1 作主语
动词不定式作主语时，可以直接位于句首（常见于正式文体），也可以用形式主语it替代。

**示例**：
- To help others gives a person satisfaction.
- To prevent harmful habits like these from dominating a teenager's life is essential.
- To change bad habits is never easy, even with many attempts.
- It was my duty to cook, so I tried to vary the meals in whatever way I could.
- It was easy to imagine the danger ahead of them.
- It's very important to master a foreign language.
- To see is to believe.

#### 2.2 作表语
动词不定式位于系动词之后，说明主语的内容或目的。

**示例**：
- Perhaps the best way to understand Western art is to look at the development of Western painting over the centuries.
- In this work, Monet's aim was to convey the light and movement in the scene.
- The most important thing for a doctor is to heal the wounded and save the dying.
- The aim of this initiative is to encourage cooperation and trade across the historic Silk Road areas.

#### 2.3 作宾语
动词不定式作及物动词的宾语，有时用it作形式宾语。

**示例**：
- Would you like to watch a football game with me?
- He finds it very difficult to stand on one foot for a long time. (it作形式宾语)
- Do you know where to find Tom? (疑问词+不定式)

#### 2.4 作定语
动词不定式作定语时，一般置于被修饰词之后，用作后置定语。

**示例**：
- Scientists were determined to help humans realise their dream to explore space.
- On 12 April 1961, Yuri Gagarin became the first person in the world to go into space.
- Sending people to other planets or even beyond the solar system is not an easy goal to achieve.
- Jessie got a new toy to play with.
- She has no friend to depend on.
- He is always the last one to leave the office.
- We are very busy and we all have a lot of work to do on weekends.
- There is an urgent need to study the Arctic in order to understand climate change and its effects.

#### 2.5 作状语
动词不定式可以表示目的、结果或原因等。

**示例**：
- Scientists work hard to find out the secrets of the universe.
- As we all know, an astronaut needs to be healthy and calm in order to work in space.
- You must be intelligent enough to get a related college degree.
- I'm afraid the whole process is too complicated to explain in a few words.
- We were surprised to find that he had already left.
- Many people rush to the new city to look for job opportunities.
- A few years later he came home to find that his hometown had greatly changed.

#### 2.6 作宾语补足语
动词不定式位于某些及物动词的宾语之后，补充说明宾语的动作或状态。

**示例**：
- She often helps her mother (to) do some housework on weekends.
- Miss Li advised us to read some English novels during the summer holiday.
- Marco Polo's tales inspired European explorers to search for sea routes from west to east.

**注意**：某些动词后用不带to的不定式构成复合宾语，但当整个句子变为被动结构时，不定式要加to。
- The boss made the workers work long hours. → The workers were made to work long hours.
- I saw you climb through the window. → You were seen to climb through the window.

### 3. "疑问词 + 不定式"结构
动词不定式可与疑问词when、where、how、what、which、who等组合，构成不定式短语，在句中主要用作主语、宾语和表语。

**示例**：
- When to start has not been decided yet. (作主语)
- Do you know where to find Tom? (作宾语)
- My main concern is how to get there on time. (作表语)

### 4. 动词不定式的时态和语态

#### 4.1 时态
- **一般时(to do)**：表示动作与谓语动作同时发生或在其后发生
- **进行时(to be doing)**：表示动作与谓语动作同时进行
- **完成时(to have done)**：表示动作在谓语动作之前完成

**示例**：
- When we arrived at the riverside, they seemed to be cooking something on the fire.
- I am sorry to have kept you waiting.
- She seems to have read the book before.
- The Dutch may have been the first Europeans to have explored Australia.

#### 4.2 语态
当句子中的不定式与其所关联的名词在语义上有被动关系时，不定式用被动语态。

**示例**：
- It was impossible for lost time to be made up.
- Can you tell me which car is to be repaired?
- I wish to be sent to work in the western region.
- There were so many passengers who needed to be transferred.
- It's a great honour for me to be invited to Mary's birthday party.
