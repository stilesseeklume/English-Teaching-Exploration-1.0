# 第3讲 其他常考短语动词和惯用表达

## 概述

*本讲讲解高考中常考的其他短语动词和惯用表达，包括一些不属于前两讲分类的重要搭配。*

## 详细内容

### 常考短语动词

| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **carry on** | 继续做 | The meal over, the managers went back to the meeting room to carry on their discussion. |
| **make up** | 组成；编造；化妆 | American Indians make up about five percent of the U.S. population. |
| **figure out** | 弄清楚，弄明白 | The present situation is very complex, so I think it will take me some time to figure out its reality. |
| **hold on** | 等一下（电话用语）；坚持 | Hold on a moment and I will go to your rescue. |
| **lead to** | 导致 | We firmly believe that war never settles anything. It only leads to violence. |
| **die down** | (风、浪等)渐渐平息 | Although the wind has died down, the rain remains steady, so you still need a raincoat. |
| **see sb. off** | 为某人送行 | John is leaving for London tomorrow and I will see him off at the airport. |
| **refer to** | 参考；提到；指的是 | The president spoke at the business meeting for nearly an hour without referring to his notes. |
| **bring up** | 抚养长大；提出 | Born into a family with three brothers, David was brought up to value the sense of sharing. |
| **keep up** | 保持；坚持 | Walking alone in the dark, the boy whistled to keep up his courage. |
| **look into** | 调查 | I'm so glad you've come here to look into this matter in person. |
| **run out** | 用完，耗尽（不及物） | Food supplies in the flood-stricken area are running out. We must act immediately before there's none left. |
| **rule out** | 排除 | The experiment has ruled out the possibility of the existence of any life on that planet. |
| **live up to** | 达到（期望） | Had she lived up to her promise, she would have made it to Yale University. |
| **fit in** | 适应；融入 | Amy joined a painting group but didn't seem to fit in, so she left. |
| **build up** | 积累；增强 | Bears build up fat stores throughout the summer and fall to have energy enough to last them through their winter sleep. |
| **make the best of** | 充分利用，尽力而为 | The school isn't the one I really wanted to go to, but I suppose I'll just have to make the best of it. |
| **make up for** | 弥补 | Caroline doesn't have a gift for music, but she makes up for it with hard work. |
| **keep it up** | 继续努力；保持下去 | I get at least half an hour of exercise almost every day. — Oh great! Keep it up. |
| **appeal to** | 吸引；呼吁 | We tend to have a better memory for things that excite our senses or appeal to our emotions than for straight facts. |
| **take advantage of** | 利用 | Parents should actively urge their children to take advantage of the opportunity to join sports teams. |
| **bring out** | 使显现；出版 | Working with the medical team in Africa has brought out the best in her as a doctor. |
| **make out** | 辨认出；理解 | Kate heard a man's voice in the background, but she couldn't make out what he was saying. |
| **work out** | 解决；算出；锻炼 | We had wanted to finish our task by noon, but it didn't quite work out. |
