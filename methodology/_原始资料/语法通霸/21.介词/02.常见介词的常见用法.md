# 第2讲 常见介词的常见用法

## 概述

*本讲详细讲解13个常见介词的重要用法，包括 against, as, at, beyond, by, for, from, in, of, off, on, to/toward, with。每个介词配有丰富的例句和高考真题。*

## 详细内容

### 考点1. against 的重要用法

#### A. 反对；违反，违背；反抗
- the fight against terrorism 反恐战争
- We're playing against Class Four next week. 下周我们和四班比赛。
- We were rowing against the wind. 我们逆风划船。
- That's against the law. 那是违背法律的。
- She was forced to marry against her will. 她被迫违背自己的意愿结婚。
- Are you for or against the plan? 你是赞成还是反对这个计划？

#### B. 不利于
The evidence is against him. 证据对他不利。

#### C. 靠着，挨着
Put the piano there, against the wall. 把钢琴放那边，靠着墙。

#### D. 映衬，衬托
The pine trees were black against the morning sky. 在早晨天空的映照下，那些松树是黑的。

#### E. 预防，防备
We are all taking medicine against the cold. 我们都服药以防感冒。

### 考点2. as 的重要用法

as 作介词时表示"作为"，如：
- Let's learn to use the problem we are facing **as** a stepping-stone to future success.
- "You can't judge a book by its cover," **as the old saying goes**.

### 考点3. at 的重要用法

常见搭配：
- **at least** 至少
- **at home** 感觉自在
- **at heart** 内心深处
- **at will** 随意
- **at sight** 一看见就

### 考点4. beyond 的重要用法

#### A. 作介词，"在那边；超出"
- The sea is beyond that hill. 大海在山的那边。
- Some shops keep open beyond midnight. 有些商店营业到半夜以后。
- These were matters beyond his understanding. 这些事情他那时候还不能理解。
- She was really touched beyond words. 她确实感动得无以言表。
- I know nothing beyond what he told me. 除了他告诉我的以外，别的我都不知道。
- He didn't believe in people living beyond 100. 他不相信人能活到100岁以上。

#### B. 作副词
If we cross the mountains we may find people living in the valley beyond. 如果我们翻过这些大山，我们就可以发现在远处山谷中生活的人们。

### 考点5. by 的重要用法

#### A. 表示做某事的方式
- They travelled across Europe by car. 他们乘车穿越欧洲。
- She finished her work (all) by herself. 她单独完成了她的作业。
- We went in by (=through) the front door. 我们从前门进来。
- Do you wish to be paid in cash or by cheque? 你是想通过现金支付还是通过支票支付？
- He learned English by listening to the radio. 他通过听收音机学英语。
- Suddenly, she grabbed him by the arm. 她突然抓住他的胳膊。
- I refuse to live by their rules. 我拒绝按他们的规则生活。

#### B. 引导时间状语，"不迟于；在"

#### C. 用于"计量"
- Our office floor space measured twelve metres by ten. 我们办公室地板面积是12米乘以10米。
- The workers are paid by the hour. 那些工人们的工作按小时支付。

#### D. 在……期间
We travelled by night and rested by day. 我们夜间旅行，白天休息。

### 考点6. for 的重要用法

#### A. 后跟一段时间或距离
- I haven't seen him for three days. 我已经三天没见他了。
- They walked for twenty miles yesterday. 昨天他们走了20英里。

#### B. 表原因，"因为"
He was put into prison for murder. 因为谋杀他被关进了监狱。

#### C. 表"赞成"，表示"反对"用 against
I am for the plan, but he is against it. 我赞成这个计划而他反对这个计划。

#### D. 对于……来说
- The ice-cream was a little bit sweet for me. 这个冰淇淋对于我来说有点甜。
- That jacket looks a bit big for you. 这个夹克对于你来说有点大。

#### E. 朝着，向着
- It seemed as if they were heading for the train station. 看起来好像他们朝车站方向走去。
- He is leaving (Beijing) for New York tomorrow. 明天他将离开（北京）去纽约。

#### F. 和 pay, sell, buy, exchange 等词连用，表交换
- She sold the house for quite a lot of money. 那座房子她卖了很多钱。
- They've said they'll repair my car for £300. 他们已经说修我的车需要300英镑。

#### G. 双宾语中用 for 的动词
He bought a book for me. / He made a kite for me.

### 考点7. from 的重要用法

**be far from** 远非，绝不（字面意思：离达到……程度很远）
- His work is far from perfect. 他的工作远非十全十美。
- He is far from well. 他根本谈不上健康。

**链接：anything but** 根本不（字面意思：任何东西都行除了……）
- The problem is anything but easy. 这个问题绝不容易。
- She is anything but a beauty. 她一点也不漂亮。
- She can do anything but sing. 她除了唱歌之外，什么都会做。

### 考点8. in 的重要用法

#### A. in 可以表示用某种颜色或笔来写
- Cheques should be written in ink. 支票应该用墨水来写。
- She usually paints in watercolor. 她通常用水彩画画。
- Was the letter written in pen? 这封信是用钢笔写的吗？

#### B. 穿着
- Do you know that man in the grey suit? 你认识那个穿灰色制服的人吗？
- You look nice in green (= green clothes). 你穿绿衣服很好看。

**常见 in 短语**：in vain 徒劳 / in store 储备着 / in the form of 以……形式 / in case 以防 / in favor of 赞成 / in the meanwhile 与此同时 / in exchange for 交换 / in detail 详细地 / in shape 健康 / in harmony with 与……和谐 / in advance 提前

### 考点9. of 的重要用法

#### A. of + 名词，使其具有形容词的性质
- This dictionary will be of great benefit to me. 这部字典将对我有很大裨益。
- He smells of tobacco. 他身上带有烟草味。
- It tastes of lemon. 这东西有柠檬味道。

#### B. 不及物动词 + of，使其具有及物动词的功能
- What will become of those refugees? 那些难民会落得什么结果呢？
- The course consists of ten core modules and five optional modules. 这门课程包括十个必修单元和五个选修单元。
- Did the people at large approve of the government's policy? 一般老百姓赞成政府的政策吗？

#### C. of 和 clear, deprive 等词连用，表示被"除去，清除，剥夺"的具体内容
- They cleared the land of trees. 他们清除了那块地上的树。
- It was the accident that deprived her of a normal life. 正是那场车祸剥夺了她的正常生活。
- She was glad to be relieved of this burden. 她很高兴卸掉了这个包袱。
- They held up the owner and robbed him of everything. 他们劫持了货主，并抢走了他的所有东西。
- The king stripped him of all his honours. 国王剥夺了他的一切荣誉。

#### D. 在 inform/remind sb. of sth. 中，of 相当于 about
- They accused him of selling out the interests of the people. 他们指责他出卖人民的利益。
- He assured me of his full support for my plan. 他向我保证全力支持我的计划。
- I convinced him of my innocence. 我使他相信我是无辜的。
- The fresh air on the farm cured him of his headache. 农场的新鲜空气治好了他的头痛病。
- They came round and informed us of their decision. 他们来把他们的决议通知了我们。
- It reminded me of the mistakes I had made before. 它使我想起了以前我犯的一些错误。
- They suspected him of murder. 他们怀疑他是杀人犯。

#### E. 在 demand/ask sth. of sb. 等表示"要求，期望"的结构中
- He asked a favour of me. 他求我一件事。
- The parents expected too much of him. 父母对他期望过高。

### 考点10. off 有脱落某个物体之意

- He jumped off the desk/horse. 他从桌子/马上跳下来。
- She fell off her bike/the tree. 她从车上/树上摔下来。

### 考点11. on 的重要用法

#### A. 身边带有
I didn't have any money on me. 我身边没有带钱。

#### B. 表示身体姿势（着眼于接触点）
- In return for this, the beggar stood on his head and sang songs. 这个乞丐倒立着唱了几支歌作为报答。
- He fell on his knees and begged for mercy. 他跪下来乞求怜悯。
- He is lying on his back/side. 他正仰卧（侧卧）着。
- He lay at full length on his stomach. 他全身伸展地俯卧着。

#### C. 表示由谁付钱
This meal is on me. 这顿饭我请客。

#### D. 表示"在……之后"
- On their return they discovered that their house had been burgled. 他们回来之后，发现他们的房子被盗了。

#### E. 表示"是某个组织或团体的成员"（英国英语用 in）
- He is by far the best player on the team. 他是该队最好的队员。
- There are no women on the committee. 这个委员会中没有女性。

#### F. 表示"挨着或沿着边界"
- The capital is located on the river. 首都位于河畔。
- Our house was on Renmin Street. 我们的房子在人民街。

#### G. 表示"以……为食/为生/为动力"
- What do mice live on? 老鼠以什么为食？
- Does this radio run on batteries? 这个收音机用的是电池吗？
- Birds feed on nuts and berries in the winter. 鸟类靠坚果和浆果过冬。

#### H. 表示"正在进行"
- He accidentally set his bed on fire. 他无意中点着了他的床。
- Martin's on holiday this week. 马丁这周度假。
- Crime is on the increase again. 犯罪率又在上升了。

#### I. have sth. on sb. 抓住某人的把柄
Don't worry; the boss has (got) nothing on us. 别着急，老板没有任何把柄来找我们的茬。

#### J. 表示"在某物或某事上"花钱或挣钱
- He spent eighty pounds on a hat. 他买这顶帽子花了80镑。
- I've wasted a lot of money on this car. 在这辆车上我已经浪费了很多钱。

#### K. wait on sb. 侍候某人
- The staff who waited on us at dinner were excellent. 晚餐上给我们服务的人员非常优秀。
- She waited on tables (= served meals as a job) to earn some extra money. 她在餐馆当服务员以挣点额外收入。

### 考点12. to 与 toward

#### A. solution, key, answer, reply, entrance, access 等后面常跟介词 to
#### B. to 还有"按照"之意
- to one's taste 按照某人的口味
- to one's measurement 按照某人的尺寸
- to the music 按照音乐节拍

#### C. be friendly/grateful/thankful to sb. for sth.
- I am extremely grateful to all the teachers for their help. 我非常感谢所有老师的帮助。
- We are thankful to you for all your assistance. 我们感谢你给予的一切帮助。

### 考点13. with 的重要用法

#### A. 在一些短语中
- slow in understanding 理解力慢
- be patient with sb. 对某人有耐心

#### B. with 表具有
The wine industry in the area has developed in a special way, **with** little foreign ownership.

#### C. with 常和 problem, help, trouble 等词连用
- William Gaddis was engaged to help with the writing. William Gaddis 也参与到帮助写作上来。
- He also needed help with repairs to the tools. 在修理那些工具上，他也需要帮助。
- He still has a serious problem with money. 他手头还是很紧。
- He began to have trouble with his right knee. 他的右膝盖开始出现不适。

#### D. end with / begin with / start with 以……结束/开始
- All of the passengers started the day with a swim. 所有的乘客那天一开始就先游了个泳。
- They ended the play with a song. 他们以一首歌曲结束了这出剧。
