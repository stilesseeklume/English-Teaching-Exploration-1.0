# 被动语态

## 概述
被动语态（The Passive Voice）表示主语是动作的承受者。与主动语态相对，被动语态强调动作的承受者而非执行者。本部分涵盖现在进行时和现在完成时的被动语态、短语动词的被动语态、双宾语和复合宾语的被动结构。

## 详细内容

### 1. 现在进行时的被动语态（the present continuous passive voice）
以动词ask为例：

| 肯定式 | 否定式 | 疑问式 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| I am being asked ... | I am not being asked ... | Am I being asked ...? |
| He/She/It is being asked ... | He/She/It is not being asked ... | Is he/she/it being asked ...? |
| We are being asked ... | We are not being asked ... | Are we being asked ...? |
| You are being asked ... | You are not being asked ... | Are you being asked ...? |
| They are being asked ... | They are not being asked ... | Are they being asked ...? |

### 2. 现在完成时的被动语态（the present perfect passive voice）
以动词ask为例：

| 肯定式 | 否定式 | 疑问式 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| I have been asked ... | I have not been asked ... | Have I been asked ...? |
| He/She/It has been asked ... | He/She/It has not been asked ... | Has he/she/it been asked ...? |
| We have been asked ... | We have not been asked ... | Have we been asked ...? |
| You have been asked ... | You have not been asked ... | Have you been asked ...? |
| They have been asked ... | They have not been asked ... | Have they been asked ...? |

### 3. 短语动词的被动语态
短语动词是固定的词组，由“动词 + 副词/介词”或“动词 + 副词 + 介词”构成，作用相当于一个动词。

- 及物动词 + 副词/介词：可用于被动结构
  - At last they put out the fire. → At last the fire was put out.
  - Have you sent for a doctor? → Has the doctor been sent for?
- 不及物动词 + 介词/副词 + 介词：相当于及物动词，也可用于被动结构
  - The boys laughed at him for the foolish mistake. → He was laughed at for the foolish mistake.
  - They looked down on peasants in the old days. → Peasants were looked down on in the old days.

**注意**：短语动词是不可分割的整体，变为被动语态时不可丢掉介词或副词。

### 4. 双宾语的被动结构
带有双宾语的句子变为被动结构时，两个宾语中的任何一个都可作被动句的主语。

- The teacher gave the student a new book.
  - The student was given a new book (by the teacher).（间接宾语作主语）
  - A new book was given to the student (by the teacher).（直接宾语作主语，间接宾语前加to）

### 5. 复合宾语的被动结构
带有复合宾语的句子变为被动结构时，宾语补足语变为主语补足语。

- Everybody called her Big Sister. → She was called Big Sister (by everybody).
- People last saw the lost girl playing near the river. → The lost girl was last seen playing near the river.

**重要规则**：主动语态中作宾语补足语的不带to的不定式，在被动语态中必须带to。
- The engineer made the robot clean the windows. → The robot was made to clean the windows.
