# 附录2 词形变化规则

## 考点1. 名词单数变复数的规则

### A. 规则变法
- 一般加-s：map—maps, girl—girls
- 以-s,-x,-ch,-sh结尾加-es：class—classes, box—boxes
- 以-f/-fe结尾变v加-es：leaf—leaves, knife—knives
- 辅音字母+y变y为i加-es：party—parties, city—cities
- 以-o结尾"两人两菜"加-es：Negro—Negroes, tomato—tomatoes, potato—potatoes

### B. 不规则变化
goose—geese, foot—feet, tooth—teeth, man—men, woman—women, child—children, mouse—mice, ox—oxen

### C. 某国人复数：中日瑞士不变英法变，其他-s加后边

## 考点2. 形容词+ly构成副词
- 一般加-ly：slow—slowly
- 以-l结尾仍加-ly：real—really
- 以-le结尾去e加-y：possible—possibly
- 辅音+y变y为i加-ly：happy—happily

## 考点3. 比较级和最高级构成规则
- 一般加-er/-est：tall→taller→tallest
- 不发音-e加-r/-st：large→larger→largest
- 重读闭音节双写：big→bigger→biggest
- 辅音+y变y为i：happy→happier→happiest
- 多音节加more/most

不规则：good→better→best, bad→worse→worst, many/much→more→most, little→less→least, far→farther/further→farthest/furthest

## 考点4. 动词过去式和过去分词构成规则
- 一般加-ed：worked, played
- 不发音e加-d：lived, liked
- 辅音+y变y为i加-ed：studied, carried
- 重读闭音节双写：stopped, planned

## 考点5. 现在分词和动名词构成规则
- 一般加-ing：thinking, studying
- 不发音-e去e加-ing：making, coming
- 重读闭音节双写：stopping, running
- -ie改为-y：dying, lying

## 考点7. 第三人称单数构成规则
- 一般加-s：speaks, learns
- 辅音+y变y为i加-es：studies
- 以s,x,ch,sh结尾加-es：teaches, washes
- 以-o结尾加-es：goes, does
