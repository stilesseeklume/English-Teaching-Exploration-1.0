# 高考英语写作句型素材集

> 来源：赢在微点二轮复习·写作专题。学生日常背诵的「池子」。
> 课堂上的范文，句型都从这里来——你背过的句子，真的用得上。

## 第一部分：应用文写作句型（赢在微点 专题一）

### 一、观点型应用文（素材 1-3，共 23 个公式）

#### 素材 1：问题、现象和利弊

##### 第 1 组：描述学习/生活问题/现象（求助信、投诉信、短文、帖子、发言稿、倡议书）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：What disappoints sb. most is... | 让某人最烦恼/担忧的是…… | What disappoints me most is the constant device failures, which lead to delays in my work. |
| 公式 2：Sb. find/feel it hard to do... | 某人发现做……很难 | Even with many attempts, I find it not easy to cook a tasty meal. |
| 公式 3：I'd like to share my view on... | 我想分享对……的看法 | I'd like to share our views on the phenomenon of some students giving nicknames to others on campus. |
| 公式 4：There is a growing concern about... | 对……的担忧日益加剧 | There is a widespread concern about the problem that many teenagers are living under stress. |

##### 第 2 组：利弊分析（短文、帖子、发言稿、反馈信）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 5：The upside/downside lies in... | 好处/弊端在于…… | One of the greatest advantages of the Internet lies in the quick and easy access to a huge amount of information. |
| 公式 6：While..., its drawbacks can not be overlooked | 尽管……，缺点不容忽视 | While online learning offers significant advantages, its drawbacks can not be overlooked. |

#### 素材 2：观点、看法、意见和理由、原因

##### 第 1 组：表达观点（建议信、短文、帖子、发言稿）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：In my opinion / From my point of view | 在我/某人看来…… | In my opinion, what or where we eat on Spring Festival Eve really doesn't matter. |
| 公式 2：I hold the firm view that... | 我坚信…… | I hold the firm view that AI is a great aid. |
| 公式 3：My view is that... | 在我看来/我认为…… | My view is that music classes are not a distraction but a vital part of education. |

##### 第 2 组：理由、原因（建议信、推荐信、感谢信、道歉信、短文、发言稿）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 4：Given that / As / Since... | 因为…… | Given that both members might not be fluent, this could minimize their motivation to improve. |
| 公式 5：So + adj. + be + 主语 + that... | 如此……以至于……（倒装句） | So important is the ocean that we should protect it for our future generations. |
| 公式 6：The reason why...is that... | ……的原因是…… | The reason why I recommend you to choose the hotel is that it is well equipped. |
| 公式 7：..., which can bridge the gap... | 定语从句补充结果 | The game encourages us to appreciate people's strengths, which can bridge the gap and build bonds between each other. |
| 公式 8：By doing..., sb. can not only...but also... | 通过……，不仅……而且…… | By adding the column, we can not only ease students' stress but also foster a sense of connection. |

#### 素材 3：重要性、建议和措施

##### 第 1 组：表达重要性（建议信、短文、帖子、发言稿、倡议书）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：It is essential/important/vital to do... | （做）……很重要 | Through video editing, we have acquired software skills, which is essential for future study and career development. |
| 公式 2：Nothing is more important than... | 没有什么比……更重要 | Nothing is more important than developing a good learning habit. |
| 公式 3：What counts/matters is that... | 重要的是…… | What counts is that we individuals do our part to protect the ocean. |
| 公式 4：We cannot emphasize...enough | 再怎么强调……也不为过 | We cannot emphasize the significance of ocean conservation too much. |

##### 第 2 组：建议和措施（建议信、劝慰信、推荐信、发言稿、短文）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 5：I suggest doing / It is suggested that... | 建议做…… | I suggest giving us the freedom to choose our partner, thus fostering better communication. |
| 公式 6：It is advisable to do / It would be more beneficial if... | ……是可取的/更有益的 | Were it possible, it would be even more beneficial if we were allowed to select our partner. |
| 公式 7：Equally important is... | 同样重要的是…… | Equally important is to have a good conversation with your parents. |
| 公式 8：It pays to do / It makes sense to do... | 做……是有裨益的/有意义的 | It makes sense to promote cultural exchange. |
| 公式 9：The top priority should be to do... | ……是头等大事 | The top priority should be to learn how to cook a simple dish. |

### 二、描述型应用文（素材 4-6，共 26 个公式）

#### 素材 4：介绍活动（告知信、邀请信、通知、报道、宣传稿）

##### 第 1 组：活动目的

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：Learning that..., I am writing to... | 书信体表目的 | Learning that you want to participate in the UN's initiative, I am writing to offer my advice. |
| 公式 2：With the aim of / Aimed at doing..., ...will be held | 活动目的前置 | With the aim of enhancing our awareness, an activity themed "Getting to Know the Plants" was launched last Sunday. |
| 公式 3：In a bid to / Aiming to..., ...organized... | 目的状语开头 | In a bid to relieve students' stress, the Student Union organized a hiking activity last Saturday. |
| 公式 4：..., whose aim is to... | 定语从句补充目的 | The English program enjoys great popularity, whose aim is to improve students' speaking ability. |

##### 第 2 组：活动时间、地点

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 5：As scheduled, ...took place at..., lasting from...to... | 时间地点 | As scheduled, the film will begin on Sunday, lasting from 5 pm to 6 pm. |
| 公式 6：Last Friday saw/witnessed... | 时间/地点+witness（高级句式） | Last Friday saw an English drama performance in the school hall. |

##### 第 3 组：活动过程、内容

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 7：The exhibition featured a variety of..., ranging from...to... | 先「面」后「点」 | There are a variety of themes we can choose from, ranging from calligraphy to paper-cuts. |
| 公式 8：The event kicked off with... Then followed... | 时间顺序式 | The event kicked off with a speech. Then followed a short film. |
| 公式 9：What appealed to sb. most was... | 主语从句（最吸引人的是……） | What appealed to visitors most was the artworks combining painting, poetry and calligraphy. |
| 公式 10：The highlight of the event was... | 活动亮点 | A highlight was the "Heart-to-Heart Wall", where students shared worries. |

##### 第 4 组：反响、评价、感受、收获、意义

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 11：This activity was widely praised by..., which not only...but also... | 反响 | This activity was widely praised by all the participants, which not only enhanced students' physical fitness but also inspired them. |
| 公式 12：...speak highly of / sing high praise for... | 评价 | We students all gave the project a thumbs-up, saying it sharpened our social skills. |
| 公式 13：...proved rewarding, which not only...but also... | 意义 | This activity proved rewarding, which not only helped the seniors but also strengthened our bond. |
| 公式 14：Not only did we..., but we also... | 收获（倒装） | Not only did we get familiar with the plants, but we also gained deeper insight into how to preserve them. |

#### 素材 5：介绍人物（申请信、推荐信、短文）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：..., a committed and patient scientist, was born in... | 人物介绍（同位语） | Tu Youyou, a committed and patient scientist, was born in Ningbo, China. |
| 公式 2：My good command of English allows me to... | 语言能力 | My good command of English allows me to interact with foreign friends with ease. |
| 公式 3：Committed and hard-working, he achieved... | 性格（形容词作状语） | Committed and hard-working, he achieved a lot in his work. |
| 公式 4：With my proficiency in..., I could... | 特长 | With my proficiency in Chinese calligraphy, I could also conduct classes for your visitors. |
| 公式 5：Having done / Experienced in..., I can... | 相关经验 | Having worked as a part-time tour guide, I am able to effectively communicate with people. |
| 公式 6：...be regarded/remembered as... | 人物评价 | He is loved and remembered as a great actor who inspired people during dark times. |

#### 素材 6：介绍事物、地理位置、景点、风俗礼仪

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：..., dating back to..., is ranked as... | 事物介绍（修饰语） | Chinese brush, dating back to more than 2,000 years ago, is ranked as one of Four Treasures of the Study. |

## 第二部分：读后续写句型（赢在微点 专题二）

### 一、8 个动作描写公式（满分策略 2）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：主语+did A, did B and did C | 动作链（营造画面感） | He hurried out from behind the bar, rushed toward Harry and seized his hand, tears in his eyes. |
| 公式 2：主语+did A+and+did B, 非谓语 C | 动作链+非谓语 | He laced up his boots, stood up, and stormed away. |
| 公式 3：非谓语 A, 主语 did B, 非谓语 C | 非谓语开头 | Dragging the heavy box across the yard, Harry felt sweat dripping down his forehead. |
| 公式 4：主语 did A, with + n. + 非谓语 | with 复合结构 | She sat at the desk, with tears streaming down her cheeks. |
| 公式 5：主语 did A, 独立主格 | 独立主格 | He stood there, his hands trembling with excitement. |
| 公式 6：副词，主语 did A | 副词开头 | Carefully, he opened the envelope and pulled out the letter. |
| 公式 7：介词短语，主语 did A | 介词短语开头 | With a deep breath, she stepped onto the stage. |
| 公式 8：Having done..., 主语 did... | 完成时非谓语 | Having finished his homework, Tom rushed out to play. |

### 二、14 个情感公式（满分策略 3）

#### 第 1 组：脸上的表情

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：A look of + 情感名词 + flashed across one's face | 神色掠过 | A look of disbelief, then a grin, spread across his face. |
| 公式 2：情感名词 + was written all over one's face | 满脸…… | Excitement and pride were written all over his face as he crossed the finish line. |

#### 第 2 组：身体反应

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 3：One's heart + pounded/raced/skipped a beat | 心跳 | Her heart pounded as she waited for the results. |
| 公式 4：A wave of + 情感名词 + washed over sb. | 情感涌上心头 | A wave of relief washed over him when he heard the good news. |
| 公式 5：Tears + welled up in one's eyes / rolled down one's cheeks | 流泪 | Tears welled up in her eyes as she read the letter. |
| 公式 6：One's face + lit up / brightened | 脸上放光 | His face lit up when he saw the birthday cake. |
| 公式 7：One's voice + trembled / cracked | 声音颤抖 | Her voice trembled with emotion as she thanked everyone. |

### 三、5 个加修饰的对话公式（满分策略 4）

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：对话 + sb. said, + 声音描写 | 对话+声音 | "I want you to listen carefully," he said, his voice low and calm. |
| 公式 2：对话 + sb. said, + 情感描写 | 对话+情感 | "I never expected this," he said, in a low, worried voice. |
| 公式 3：对话 + sb. said, + 动作描写 | 对话+动作 | "Let's go," she said, grabbing her coat and heading for the door. |
| 公式 4：Sb. said in a(n) + 情感形容词 + voice, "..." | 前置修饰 | She said in a gentle voice, "Everything will be fine." |
| 公式 5："...," sb. + 动词（代替 said）| 高级「说」动词 | "I'm sorry," he whispered/apologized/admitted. |

### 四、6 个内心活动公式（满分策略 5）

#### 第 1 组：让某人想到/遗忘

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：It struck/occurred to sb. that... | 某人突然想到 | It struck them that they could make breakfast for Mother to celebrate Mother's Day. |
| 公式 2：Sth. crossed/flashed through sb.'s mind | 某人突然想起 | A thought crossed her mind — maybe she was wrong. |

#### 第 2 组：内心独白与思考

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 3：主语 + wondered/asked oneself if/whether... | 暗自思忖 | She wondered if she had made the right decision. |
| 公式 4：It dawned on sb. that... | 某人逐渐意识到 | It dawned on me that I had been wrong all along. |
| 公式 5：Sb. couldn't help but wonder/think... | 不禁想 | He couldn't help but wonder what would have happened if he had tried harder. |
| 公式 6：The thought that... + 谓语 | ……的念头…… | The thought that he might fail never crossed his mind. |

### 五、12 个吸睛结尾公式（满分策略 6）

#### 第 1 组：4 个结尾感悟公式

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 1：Looking back, I realized that...not only...but also... | 回顾感悟 | Looking back, I came to the realization that true education is not about standardized answers, but about nurturing courage to think. |
| 公式 2：Not until then did sb. realize that... | 倒装感悟 | Not until then did they learn the importance of trust and communication. |
| 公式 3：It was at that moment that I realized... | 强调句感悟 | It was at that moment that I realized I should not decide a person by their reputation, but by who they are. |
| 公式 4：对比反差/排比 | 高级修辞 | We usually think that our lives are defined by great moments. But I now realise that great moments catch us by surprise, because they often seem quite small. |

#### 第 2 组：2 个呼应主旨公式

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 5：...realized that... + 原文主旨句 | 呼应主旨 | Nodding happily, I realized that Gunter's kindness had not only got me to my destination but also turned a cold evening into a warm one. |
| 公式 6：...was right: ... + 感悟 | 呼应人物话语 | He was absolutely right. Knowing the limits isn't an indication of weakness. It's actually a sign of strength. |

#### 第 3 组：3 个主题升华公式

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 7：Looking back on this experience, ...reminded me that... | 经历回顾 | This experience reshapes my perspective, reminding me that family isn't just shared blood — it's about sharing laughter and caring about each other. |
| 公式 8：It dawned on me that only by...can we... | 哲理感悟 | It dawned on me that only by being a humble and inclusive listener can we achieve great success; after all, many hands make light work. |
| 公式 9：Not until then did I realize what...was | 点题式 | Not until then did I realize what true friendship was: A friend in need is a friend indeed. |

#### 第 4 组：2 个情景交融 + 1 个画面定格公式

| 公式 | 功能 | 例句（背） |
|------|------|-----------|
| 公式 10：Despite the bone-chilly wind, I felt a wave of warmth... | 静态场景+融情于景 | Despite the bone-chilly wind, I felt a wave of warmth and pride. |
| 公式 11：..., the setting sun casting two long shadows... | 动态场景+寓情于景 | Supporting each other, they both got up and headed home, the setting sun casting two long shadows on the ground, side by side. |
| 公式 12：With...in one hand and...in the other, ... | 画面定格式 | With a basket of strawberries in one hand and a heart full of joy, she skipped back home. |

## 第三部分：范文句型溯源

> 我们的范文里每个亮点句型，都能在上面找到对应的公式。
> 这说明：你平时背的「池子」，写作文时真的用得上。

### 应用文范文（邀请信）句型溯源

| 范文句子 | 对应公式 | 来源 |

## 使用说明

1. **日常积累**：每个公式配一个「背」句和一个「仿」句，构成你自己的「池子」
2. **写作时**：对照提纲上的句型类型提示（主语从句 / 倒装句 / 定语从句……），从池子中提取最合适的句型
3. **考后复盘**：对照此表，看看范文里的亮点句型，哪些公式你已经掌握了，哪些还需要加强

> 关键理念：句型不是死记硬背的——是按「表达功能」组织的。当你需要「表达结果」「表达举例」「表达目的」时，你知道去哪里找。
