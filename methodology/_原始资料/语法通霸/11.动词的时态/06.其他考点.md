# 其他考点

## 考点1. 主将从现

### A. 主句表示将来动作或状态有三种情况

主句为一般将来时、主句是祈使句、主句含有情态动词时，从句（时间、条件、让步等状语从句）用一般现在时表示将来。

- 如果你明天有空请来我家。

### B. 在时间状语从句和条件状语从句中

当主句为将来时态或表示将来意义时，时间状语从句和条件状语从句用一般现在时表将来。

- It **won't be** long before we celebrate the New Year's Day.
- He will come to call on you the moment he **finishes** his work.
- I really don't think Tony will be upset, but I will go and see him in case he **is**.
- — Mum, I was wondering if you could lend me a few dollars until I **get paid** on Friday.
- My parents have promised to come to see me before I **leave** for Africa.
- Unless extra money **is found**, the theatre will close.
- By the time Jane **gets** home, her aunt will have left for London to attend a meeting.
- I wonder if I **will have** time. If I **have** time, I'll go with you.
- Only when he apologizes for his rudeness **will I speak** to him again.

### C. 在让步状语从句中

当主句为将来时态或表示将来意义时，让步状语从句也必须用一般现在时表示将来。

- I'll follow him wherever he goes. 他去哪儿，我就跟着去哪儿。
- Whatever you say, I won't believe you. 无论你说什么，我都不会相信你。
- I won't go to the party even if he invites me. 即使他邀请我，我也不会去参加那个派对。

### D. 在定语从句中

当主句为将来时态时，定语从句也通常用一般现在时表将来。

- I'll give you anything you ask for. 你要什么我都给你。
- Anyone who comes first will get a present. 任何人先到都可得到一份礼物。

## 考点2. 时间状语从句用一般现在时表示将来时，也可用完成时

- You won't know if the coat fits you unless you **have tried** it on.
- We won't start the work until all the preparations **have been made**.
- In my hometown, there is always a harvest supper for the farmers after all the wheat **has been** cut.

## 考点3. by 引导时间状语的时态规律

by 引导时间状语时意为"在……之前，到……为止"，其时态规律如下：

### A. by 和表示过去的时间连用时，句子常用过去完成时

- By the time Jack returned home from England, his son **had graduated** from college.
- Silk **had become** one of the primary goods traded along the Silk Road by about 100 BC.

### B. by 和表示现在的时间 now 连用，句子常用现在完成时

- The construction of the two new railway lines **has been completed** by now.

### C. by 和表示将来的时间连用时，句子常用将来完成时

- The mayor of Beijing says that all construction work for the Beijing Olympics **will have been completed** by 2008.
- By this time next year, we **will have turned** all the land into rice fields.

### D. by the time 引导的时间状语从句常用一般现在时表示将来时

- You needn't hurry her. She **will have finished** it by the time you are ready.
- He'll be an English teacher by the time he **is** twenty.
- By the time you have finished this book, your meal **will get** cold.

### E. by the time 还有一种用法，表示"在……时候"，相当于 at the time

by 的英语解释为：not later than; at or before。

- By around 900 AD, there were many places in Northern Europe where the Vikings chose to live.

- **It** is often the case with children, Amy was better by the time the doctor arrived.

## 考点4. 祈使句用动词原形

- — Sorry, Joe. I didn't mean to...
  — Don't call me "Joe". I'm Mr. Parker to you, and **don't** you forget it.
- Oh, it's so cold in here. Somebody **close** the window, please.
- **Observe** carefully if any change occurs when doing experiments in the lab.
- Every day **read** a proverb aloud several times until you have it memorized.

## 考点6. It is/was the first time that + 现在/过去完成时

- It is the first time I have seen a foreigner.
  这是我第一次见外国人。（前面是 is，后用现在完成时）
- It was the second time that I had met him.
  那是我第二次遇到他。（前面是 was，后用过去完成时）

- — Do you know our town at all?
  — No, this is the first time I **have been** here.
- That was the first time I **had seen** this kind of refrigerator.
- This is the first time we **have seen** a film in the cinema together as a family.

## 考点7. it's two years since... 也可以说 it has been two years since...

be 可以用现在时，也可以用完成时。

- It is / has been five years since I left there.
- It is almost five years **since** we saw each other last time.

## 考点8. It's three years since he smoked. 怎么翻译

It's three years since he smoked. 应译为"他已经三年没吸烟了"，而不是"他吸烟已经三年了"。因为 smoked 用的是过去式，表明吸烟是过去的事，现在已经不吸了。从过去吸烟时候算起到现在三年，就是"他不吸烟已经三年了"。since 后面从句中动词是延续性的则要从结束算起。

- It's two years since you taught here. 你不在这里教学已经两年了。

- — Why haven't I seen Mary recently?
  — Oh, because it is two weeks since she **lived** here.

- It is five years **since** he worked here.
