# 第3讲 作宾语

## 概述
本讲详细讲解非谓语动词（动词不定式和动名词）作宾语的各种用法规则，包括只能跟不定式作宾语的动词、只能跟动名词作宾语的动词、两者皆可用但意义相同或不同的动词、特殊结构中的宾语用法等核心语法知识。

## 详细内容

### 考点1. 有些动词只能跟不定式作宾语
这类及物动词要求宾语必须使用动词不定式形式，不能使用动名词。

**常见动词列表**：
- agree 同意
- attempt 试图
- afford 付得起
- ask 要求
- apply 申请
- promise 答应
- care 关心
- choose 选择
- decide 决定
- desire 渴望
- determine 决心
- help 帮助
- expect 期望
- fail 未能
- hope 希望
- intend 打算
- learn 学习
- manage 设法
- offer 提供
- plan 打算
- pretend 假装
- refuse 拒绝
- wish 希望

**语法规则**：
- 这些动词后必须接动词不定式作宾语，不能接动名词
- 不定式的逻辑主语通常与主句主语一致
- 不定式可以带自己的宾语、状语等成分

### 考点2. 有些动词只能跟动名词作宾语
这类及物动词要求宾语必须使用动名词形式，不能使用动词不定式。

**常见动词列表**：
- dislike 不喜欢
- forbid 禁止
- deny 否认
- avoid 避免
- advise 建议
- discuss 讨论
- forgive 原谅
- permit 允许
- fancy 设想
- keep 保持
- practice 练习
- put off 推迟
- stop 停止
- miss 错过
- suggest 建议
- admit 承认
- give up 放弃
- appreciate 感激
- finish 完成
- mention 提及
- prevent 阻止
- delay 推迟
- feel like 想做某事
- escape 逃脱
- consider 考虑
- excuse 原谅
- allow 允许
- enjoy 喜爱
- imagine 想象
- mind 介意
- prohibit 禁止
- risk 冒险

**语法规则**：
- 这些动词后必须接动名词作宾语，不能接动词不定式
- 动名词可以有自己的逻辑主语（用所有格形式表示）
- 动名词可以有时态和语态的变化

### 考点3. 有些动词既可跟不定式也可跟动名词作宾语，意思上差别不大
这类动词后接不定式或动名词作宾语时，基本意义相同或相近。

**常见动词**：start, begin, continue, prefer, bother 等

**示例**：
- He began to work/working as soon as he got there.
- I'd prefer not to discuss this issue.
- He prefers watching rugby to playing it.
- You'd have found it if you'd bothered looking/to look.

**特殊规则**：
- 如果 start 和 begin 用于进行时或后面跟 know, understand 作宾语，宾语要用不定式：
  - The students are starting to work on the difficult maths problem.
  - I soon began to understand what was happening.

### 考点4. 有些动词既可跟不定式也可跟动名词作宾语，但意思上有差别
这类动词后接不定式或动名词作宾语时，意义有明显区别。

**A. remember 记得**
- remember doing/having done：记得以前曾经做过某事（过去经历）
- remember to do：记着要做某事（将来动作）

**B. forget 忘记**
- forget doing/having done：忘了以前曾经做过某事（过去经历）
- forget to do：忘记做某事（应做而未做）

**C. regret 后悔/遗憾**
- regret doing sth.：对做过的某事表示后悔（过去动作）
- regret to say/tell/inform：很抱歉地说/告诉/通知（现在动作）

**D. mean 意味着/打算**
- mean to do：打算做某事（意图）
- mean doing：意味着（表示某种结果或含义）

**E. try 尝试/尽力**
- try to do：尽力做某事（努力尝试）
- try doing：尝试着做某事（试验性尝试）

**F. want/need/require 需要**
- want/need/require doing sth.：跟动名词表示被动 = want/need/require to be done
  - The grass needs cutting. = The grass needs to be cut.

**G. be/get used to 习惯于 vs used to 过去经常**
- be/get used to doing：习惯于做某事（现在习惯）
- used to do：过去经常做某事（过去习惯）
- be used to do：被用来做（被动语态）

**H. can't help 忍不住/不能帮助**
- can't help doing = can't help but do：忍不住做某事（情不自禁）
- can't help (to) do：不能帮助做某事（此时 to 可省略）

**I. stop 停止**
- stop doing：停止正在做着的某事（停止当前动作）
- stop to do：停下来做别的事情（to do 是目的状语）

**J. go on 继续**
- go on doing：继续做原来做的事（同一动作持续）
- go on to do：接着干别的事情（转换到新动作）

**K. like/hate 喜欢/讨厌**
- like/hate doing：喜欢/讨厌某种行为（经常性、一般性）
- would like/hate to do：喜欢/讨厌去做某事（特定的某一次）

### 考点5. except/but 后跟不定式
在 except, but 后跟不定式时，如果前面有实义动词 do 的某种形式（do, does, did, doing 等），不定式不带 to；否则要带 to。

**规则**：
- 前有 do 形式：不定式省略 to
- 前无 do 形式：不定式带 to

**示例**：
- There is nothing to do except wait till it stops raining.
- Standing on the top of the hill, I would not do anything but enjoy the flowing of the smog around me.

### 考点6. remain to be done
remain 后跟不定式表示"有待……"、"尚需……"，常用被动形式 remain to be done。

**结构**：remain + to be done
**意义**：表示某事有待完成或有待观察

**示例**：
- It remains to be seen whether Jim will be fit enough to play in the finals.

### 考点7. 介词后跟动名词作宾语
介词后面必须使用动名词作宾语，不能使用动词不定式。

**基本规则**：
- 所有介词后都跟动名词作宾语
- 某些动词短语中的介词也要跟动名词

**常见介词短语**：
- prevent...from doing 阻止……做某事
- look forward to doing 盼望做某事
- be used to doing 习惯于做某事
- object to doing 反对做某事
- devote...to doing 致力于做某事
- pay attention to doing 注意做某事
- get down to doing 开始认真做某事

### 考点8. 含有介词to的常考短语
英语中有许多含有介词 to 的固定短语，后面必须跟动名词作宾语。

**A. be addicted to 沉溺于；对……上瘾**
- He is addicted to gambling. 他沉溺于赌博。

**B. devote oneself to doing sth./be devoted to doing sth. 献身于做某事/专心致力于做某事**
- She devoted herself to helping the poor.
- Her life was devoted to caring for the sick and needy.

**C. be equal to doing sth. 等于做某事，能胜任做某事**
- This is equal to saying that he knows her well.
- She didn't feel equal to receiving visitors.

**D. object to doing sth. 反对做某事**
- have an objection to doing sth. 反对（反感）做某事
- He objected to being treated like a child.

**E. be opposed to doing sth. 反对做某事**
- I'm opposed to telling him the news at once.

**F. reduce sb. to doing sth. 使某人沦为……**
- Hunger reduced them to stealing.
- He was reduced to begging for food.

**G. pay attention to doing sth. 注意做某事**
- We should pay more attention to uniting and working with the comrades who differ with us.

**H. be used to doing sth./be accustomed to doing sth. 习惯于做某事**
- John has got used to sleeping late.
- I am not accustomed to being treated like this.

**I. get down to doing sth. 开始做某事，认真处理某事**
- After lunch we got down to discussing the issue of pay.

**J. look forward to doing sth. 盼望做某事**
- I look forward to hearing from you.

**K. come/be near/close to doing sth. 几乎快要做某事**
- I was near to screaming.
- We had an outstanding season and came so close to winning the title again.

**L. in addition to doing sth. 除做某事之外**
- In addition to giving me some advice, he gave me some money.

**M. key to doing sth. 做某事的关键**
- Yet transportation is the key to getting this food to the children and their families.

**N. make contributions to 对……做贡献**
- Everyone should make a contribution to protecting the environment.

**O. when it comes to... 谈到……时**
- There is no shortage of ideas when it comes to improving the education of children.

**P. on one's way to doing... 处在去做某事的过程中**
- Alice just got into medical school. She's on her way to becoming a doctor.

## 核心要点总结

### 宾语用法分类要点
1. **只接不定式**：agree, decide, hope 等约20个动词
2. **只接动名词**：enjoy, avoid, finish 等约30个动词
3. **两者皆可，意义相同**：begin, start, continue, prefer 等
4. **两者皆可，意义不同**：remember, forget, regret, try, mean 等10+个动词

### 特殊结构要点
1. **except/but 后不定式**：前有 do 形式省略 to，否则带 to
2. **remain to be done**：表示"有待……"的固定结构
3. **介词后必须用动名词**：所有介词后都跟动名词

### 含有介词to的短语要点
1. **to 为介词的短语**：look forward to, be used to, object to 等
2. **必须跟动名词**：这些短语中的 to 是介词，不是不定式符号
3. **常见误用**：将介词 to 误认为不定式符号而使用动词原形

### 易错点辨析
1. **动名词逻辑主语**：用所有格形式表示（your calling）
2. **不定式时态语态**：根据语境选择 to do, to be doing, to have done 等
3. **被动意义表达**：need doing = need to be done
4. **习惯 vs 过去**：be used to doing（习惯） vs used to do（过去经常）

### 学习建议
1. 分类记忆动词列表，掌握基本搭配规则
2. 重点区分意义不同的动词（remember, forget 等）
3. 掌握介词 to 短语，避免与不定式混淆
4. 在实际语境中分析宾语的选择和意义
