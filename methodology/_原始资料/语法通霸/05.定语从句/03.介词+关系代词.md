# 第3讲 介词/关系代词

## 概述
本讲深入讲解定语从句中“介词+关系代词”结构的用法规则，包括介词与关系代词的搭配限制、特殊结构“from where”和“since when”、固定短语动词中介词的位置、“介词+关系代词”作后置定语时的移位现象，以及介词选择的三大原则（看先行词搭配、看谓语搭配、看句子意思）。通过大量真题和练习，掌握复杂定语从句的构造技巧。

## 详细内容

### 考点1. 关系代词直接跟在介词后时，只可用 whom 或 which，不可用 who, that
- 当介词位于关系代词之前时（介词+关系代词结构），关系代词只能用 **whom**（指人）或 **which**（指物）。
- **不能用 who 或 that**。
- 如果介词不提前，关系代词位于定语从句句首，则可用 who/whom/that/which，也可省略。

**示例**：
```
This is the man to whom I spoke. （正确）
This is the man to who I spoke.  （错误）
This is the man to that I spoke. （错误）

This is the man (who/whom/that) I spoke to. （正确，介词后置）
```

**高考真题**：
1. 【2016浙江】The study suggests that the cultures we grow up ______ influence the basic processes by which we see world around us.
   A. on  B. in  C. at  D. about  
   **答案**：B（in which，介词 in 与 cultures 搭配）

2. 【2020江苏】Many lessons are now available online, from ______ students can choose for free.
   A. that  B. which  C. when  D. whom  
   **答案**：B（which，介词 from 后只能用 which）

### 考点2. from where, since when 为“介词+关系副词”结构，可以引导定语从句
- **from where**：表示“从那里”，引导定语从句修饰地点名词。
- **since when**：表示“从那时起”，引导定语从句修饰时间名词。
- 这两种结构本质上是“介词+关系副词”，关系副词 where/when 已包含地点/时间意义，前面再加介词表示方向或起点。

**示例**：
```
China is the birthplace of kites, from where kite flying spread to Japan, Korea, Thailand and India.
（from where 修饰 birthplace，表示“从风筝的发源地”）

The book was written in 1946, since when the education system has witnessed great changes.
（since when 修饰 1946，表示“自1946年以来”）
```

**练习**：
1. China is the birthplace of kites, ______ kite flying spread to Japan, Korea, Thailand and India.
   A. from that  B. from where  C. from there  D. from here  
   **答案**：B（from where）

2. 【2007山东】The book was written in 1946, ______ the education system has witnessed great changes.
   A. when  B. during which  C. since then  D. since when  
   **答案**：D（since when）

### 考点3. look after, look for 等固定短语动词
- 在定语从句中，固定短语动词（如 look after, look for, take care of 等）一般不宜将介词与动词分开。
- 即：介词应紧跟动词，不要提前到关系代词前。
- 如果提前，短语动词的完整性被破坏，可能改变意思或造成语法错误。

**示例**：
```
This is the baby whom she has taken care of. （正确，介词 of 后置）
This is the baby of whom she has taken care. （别扭，但语法上可接受）
This is the baby after whom she looks. （错误，look after 是固定短语，不宜拆开）
```

**练习**：
The old man has three sons, all ______ are working abroad.
A. of whom  B. of them  C. who  D. whom  
**答案**：A（of whom，表示“其中”）

### 考点4. “介词+关系代词”作后置定语时，可移到所修饰词之前
- 当“介词+关系代词”在定语从句中作后置定语（修饰从句中的某个名词）时，可以将其移到所修饰词之前，使句子更紧凑。
- 常见结构：**名词 + of which/whom**。

**示例**：
```
The house the windows of which are broken is empty.
= The house of which the windows are broken is empty.
（of which 修饰 windows，可移至 windows 前）

I have many friends, some of whom are businessmen.
（of whom 修饰 some，表示“其中一些”）
```

**高考真题**：
1. 【2008四川】For many cities in the world, there is no room to spread out further, ______ New York is an example.
   A. for which  B. in which  C. of which  D. from which  
   **答案**：C（of which，表示“其中”）

2. 【2012四川】In our class there are 46 students, ______ half wear glasses.
   A. in whom  B. for whom  C. of whom  D. of them  
   **答案**：C（of whom，表示“其中”）

3. 【2004广西】The journey around the world took the old sailor nine months, ______ the sailing time was 226 days.
   A. of which  B. during which  C. from which  D. for which  
   **答案**：A（of which，表示“其中”）

### 考点5. 介词的选择：看前看后看意思
选择介词时，需要综合考虑三个因素：与先行词的搭配、与谓语动词的搭配、以及句子的整体意思。

#### A. 看与先行词的搭配
介词需与先行词构成合理的搭配关系。

**示例**：
```
The world ______ we live is made up of matter.
A. in that  B. on which  C. where we live in  D. we live in
```
**答案**：B（on which，live on the world 搭配不当，应为 live in the world，但选项 B 是 on which，故排除；实际上正确选项是 D we live in，但题目有误。典型搭配：the world in which we live）

**练习**：
1. The bicycle ______ he often rides needs repairing.
   A. on which  B. in which  C. by which  D. with which  
   **答案**：A（on which，ride on a bicycle）

2. We went through a period ______ communications were very difficult in the rural areas.
   A. which  B. whose  C. in which  D. with which  
   **答案**：C（in which，in a period）

#### B. 看谓语的搭配
介词需与定语从句中的谓语动词构成固定搭配。

**示例**：
```
The two things ______ they felt very proud are Jim's gold watch and Della's hair.
A. about which  B. of which  C. in which  D. for which
```
**答案**：B（of which，feel proud of 是固定搭配）

**练习**：
1. He is a man of great experience, ______ much can be learned.
   A. who  B. that  C. from which  D. from whom  
   **答案**：D（from whom，learn from sb.）

#### C. 根据句子意思
介词需符合句子的逻辑意义。

**示例**：
```
The clever boy made a hole in the wall, ______ he could see ______ was going on inside the house.
A. which; what  B. through which; what  C. through that; what  D. what; that
```
**答案**：B（through which; what，through the hole 表示“透过洞”，what 引导宾语从句）

**练习**：
1. In the office I never seem to have time until after 5:30 p.m., ______ many people have gone home.
   A. whose time  B. that  C. on which  D. by which time  
   **答案**：D（by which time，表示“到那时”）

2. 【2008上海春】Villagers here depend on the fishing industry, ______ there won't be much work.
   A. where  B. that  C. by which  D. without which  
   **答案**：D（without which，表示“没有它”）

## 核心要点总结

### “介词+关系代词”使用规则
1. **介词提前时**：关系代词只能用 whom（人）或 which（物），不能用 who/that。
2. **固定短语动词**：不宜拆开，介词通常后置。
3. **特殊结构**：from where（从那里）、since when（自那时起）可引导定语从句。
4. **后置定语移位**：“名词 + of which/whom”结构可使表达更简洁。
5. **介词选择三原则**：
   - 看先行词搭配（与先行词构成合理搭配）
   - 看谓语搭配（与从句谓语动词固定搭配）
   - 看句子意思（符合逻辑意义）

### 常见搭配总结
1. **介词与先行词**：
   - in the world → in which
   - on the bicycle → on which
   - during the period → during which
   - of the students → of whom
2. **介词与谓语动词**：
   - be proud of → of which/whom
   - learn from → from whom
   - look after → after whom（不宜提前）
   - depend on → on which
3. **特殊搭配**：
   - some/many/few/all/both/neither/either + of whom/which

## 学习建议
1. **掌握基本规则**：牢记介词提前时只能用 whom/which。
2. **固定短语识别**：识别常见短语动词，避免拆散。
3. **移位结构练习**：多练习“名词 + of which/whom”结构的转换。
4. **介词选择训练**：从搭配、动词、意思三个角度综合判断介词。
5. **真题分析**：研究高考真题中“介词+关系代词”的考查方式，总结常见搭配。
6. **写作应用**：在写作中尝试使用“介词+关系代词”结构，提升句子复杂度。
