2025年全国一卷 · 语法填空
An exhibition at the Jiushi Art Museum in Shanghai is featuring artwork inspired by Go, or weiqi in Chinese, which originated in China more than 4,000 years ago.

"We hope to present (present) the rather abstract Go game and AI in a visual context, and initiate dialogues with minimalist art, conceptual art and expressionism," says Tu Ningning, who is in charge of the exhibition.

"You try to lead the opponent into your trap and force them to follow your guidance (guide) till they lose," explains Wang Wei, a Go player. "The players' personalities are revealed (reveal) during the game, and one's weaknesses are exposed to the opponent."
