# 过去分词

## 概述
过去分词是英语非谓语动词的一种，形式上由"动词原形 + -ed"构成（不规则动词有特殊变化）。过去分词一般表示完成的和被动的动作，在句中可用作定语、宾语补足语、表语和状语，但不能单独构成谓语。

## 详细内容

### 1. 过去分词的基本概念
- **构成**：规则动词由动词原形加-ed构成；不规则动词有特殊变化（参见不规则动词表）
- **含义**：一般表示完成的和被动的动作
- **非谓语特性**：不能单独作谓语，但可用于完成时态（have/had + 过去分词）和被动语态（be + 过去分词）中构成谓语

### 2. 过去分词的句法功能

#### 2.1 作定语
- **单个过去分词**：一般放在名词前面
- **过去分词短语**：一般放在名词后面

**示例**：
- Before the show, hundreds of excited visitors waited in their seats eagerly.
- The Normans ruled England after the well-known Battle of Hastings in the 11th century.
- ... and the oldest castle built by the Norman rulers in Windsor nearby.
- Come and read the poem written by an eight-year-old boy!
- When the delayed flight takes off depends mainly on the weather.
- Can you tell the differences between spoken language and written language?
- The players selected from the whole country are expected to bring us honour in this summer's games.

#### 2.2 作宾语补足语
常用于使役动词（have, get, make, keep）和感官动词（see, watch, hear）等动词的宾语后面，表达谓语动词引起的宾语的变化，或补充说明宾语的性状。

**示例**：
- I felt myself often confused at first.
- She had her painting boxed so it was delivered safely.
- We found ourselves shocked by the large number of visitors.
- When speaking or writing to someone, do your best to make yourself understood.
- Owen got his nose hurt when he played football with his teammates.
- I heard the door pulled open.

#### 2.3 作表语
在be, become, get, feel, look, seem等系动词之后，表示主语的特征或性状。

**示例**：
- The audience were amazed by the beautiful music the six-year-old pianist played.
- My cousin is very interested in painting.
- She is married.
- The other children looked neglected.
- The quarrel the night before seemed forgotten.

#### 2.4 作状语
修饰谓语，说明动作发生的时间、原因、条件、方式等。过去分词作状语，用于句首比在其他位置更为常见。

**示例**：
- Born in the USA on 2 January 1970, Whitacre began studying music at the University of Nevada in 1988.
- Moved by this music, he said, "It was like seeing color for the first time."
- Well known as a successful band, the Impact members show quite a few striking abilities.
- United we stand, divided we fall.
- Seen from the top of the Tiger Hill, the city of Suzhou was very beautiful.
- Encouraged by her English teacher, Lisa soon caught up with her classmates.
- Greatly shocked, Jeff couldn't speak a word.

### 3. 过去分词与动词-ing形式的比较

#### 3.1 作表语时的区别
- **过去分词**：与其逻辑主语之间一般是被动关系
- **动词-ing形式**：表示主动关系

**示例**：
- The girls were amazed to see such an open country. (过去分词，被动)
- The farms covered a very large area, which was amazing. (动词-ing形式，主动)
- Do you feel frightened when going into the wilderness alone? (过去分词，被动)
- Going into the wilderness alone can be frightening. (动词-ing形式，主动)

#### 3.2 作状语时的区别
- **过去分词**：与其逻辑主语之间是被动关系，往往含有完成义
- **动词-ing形式**：表示主动关系

**示例**：
- Seen from the top of the mountain, the scenery was really fascinating. (过去分词，被动)
- Seeing the scenery from the top of the mountain, I was fascinated by the autumn colours. (动词-ing形式，主动)
- Finally, the company—headed by its new manager—started to make a profit. (过去分词，被动)
- Heading eastwards, you will pass the Canadian Rockies. (动词-ing形式，主动)
