# 第5讲 其他有关关系代词选择的规则

## 概述
本讲补充讲解定语从句中关系代词选择的其他重要规则，包括只能用 which/who/whom 而不用 that 的情况、whose 指人与指物的用法、指人先行词与 who 的搭配、what 不能作关系代词的限制、which 指人的特殊情形等。这些规则进一步完善关系代词的选择体系，帮助解决疑难情况。

## 详细内容

### 考点1. 只用 which, who, whom 不用 that 的情况

#### A. 关系代词直接放在介词后面时，要用 which，不用 that；要用 whom，不用 who
- 当介词位于关系代词之前（介词+关系代词结构）时，指物用 **which**，指人用 **whom**。
- 不能用 that 或 who。

**示例**：
1. Many young people, most **of whom** were well-educated, headed for remote regions to chase their dreams.
2. The growing speed of a plant is influenced by a number of factors, most **of which** are beyond our control.
3. Scientists have advanced many theories about why human beings cry tears, none **of which** has been proved.
4. Have you sent thank-you notes to the relatives from **whom** you received gifts?

**注意**：介词后不能用 that（考点1已强调）。

#### B. 引导非限制性定语从句时，要用 which, who, whom，不用 that，也不能省略
- 非限制性定语从句对先行词进行补充说明，用逗号与主句隔开。
- 关系代词不能用 that，也不能省略。

**示例**：
1. As a child, Jack studied in a village school, **which** is named after his grandfather.
2. That evening, **which** I will tell you more about later, I ended up working very late.

**对比**：
- 限制性定语从句：The book **that/which** you lent me is interesting. （that/which 可换，可省略）
- 非限制性定语从句：This book, **which** you lent me, is interesting. （只能用 which，不能省略）

### 考点2. whose 可指人也可指物
- **whose** 既可指人，也可指物，在定语从句中作定语，表示所属关系。
- 指物时，whose 可与 of which 互换；指人时，一般不与 of whom 互换（因为指人时常用名词所有格，不用 of 属格）。

**示例**：
**指物**：
1. He lives in a house **whose** window faces south.
   = He lives in a house, the window **of which** faces south.
   = He lives in a house, **of which** the window faces south.

2. I saw some trees **whose** leaves were black with disease.

3. The old temple, **whose** roof was damaged in storm, is now under repair.

4. A company **whose** profits from home markets are declining may seek opportunities abroad.

5. In 1963 the UN set up the World Food Program, one **of whose** purposes is to relieve worldwide starvation.

**指人**：
- He is the farmer **whose** son is studying in Tsinghua University.
  （不能说：He is the farmer, the son of whom...，因为指人时常用 whose）

### 考点3. 当先行词为 one, ones, anyone, everyone, none, all 指人时，关系代词常用 who
- 当先行词是 **one, ones, anyone, everyone, none, all** 且指人时，关系代词常用 **who**，较少用 that。
- 注意：这些词指物时仍用 that/which。

**示例**：
1. One **who** doesn't work hard will not succeed.
2. Anyone **who** breaks the law will be punished.
3. Everyone **who** attended the meeting agreed with the proposal.
4. All **who** heard the news were shocked.

### 考点4. what 不能作关系代词；one 作同位语
- **what** 不能作为关系代词引导定语从句。what 引导名词性从句，相当于“the thing(s) that”。
- 当定语从句的先行词需要泛指“一个……的东西/事情”时，可用 **one** 作同位语，后接定语从句。

**示例**：
**what 的错误用法**：
- Meeting my uncle after all these years was an unforgettable moment, **what** I will always treasure. （错误）
  正确：Meeting my uncle after all these years was an unforgettable moment, **one** that I will always treasure.

**one 作同位语**：
1. Meeting my uncle after all these years was an unforgettable moment, **one** (that) I will always treasure.
2. For Tim this was the beginning of a new life, **one** (that) he thought he would never see.
3. Helping others is a habit, **one** (that) you can learn even at an early age.
4. We choose this hotel because the price for a night here is down to $20, half of **what** it used to charge. （此处 what 引导宾语从句，不是定语从句）

### 考点5. 强调人的职业、某种特性、品质或才能时，which 也可代指人；先行词是婴儿、小孩时也用 which
- 当强调人的职业、身份、特性、品质或才能时，关系代词可用 **which** 指人。
- 当先行词是婴儿、小孩时，也常用 which 指代（因其性别可能未知或不强调）。

**示例**：
1. Her mother is a teacher, **which** is most glorious under the sun. （which 指 teacher 这个职业）
2. My father is a gentleman in business **which** my uncle isn't. （which 指 gentleman 这种品质）
3. The salesman trained himself for months to speak like a professor **which** he wasn't. （which 指 professor 这个身份）
4. The baby **which** is lying there has been crying for about twenty minutes. （which 指婴儿）

**练习**：
1. The key to the success of any film is finding a capable and qualified artistic director, ______ I always hope to be.
   A. who  B. that  C. what  D. which  
   **答案**：D（which，指 artistic director 这个职业身份）

2. Her sister has become a lawyer, ______ she wanted to be.
   A. who  B. that  C. what  D. which  
   **答案**：D（which，指 lawyer 这个职业）

## 核心要点总结

### 关系代词选择特殊规则
1. **介词后**：which（物）/whom（人），不用 that/who。
2. **非限制性定语从句**：which/who/whom，不用 that，不省略。
3. **whose**：可指人/物；指物时可与 of which 互换，指人时一般不用 of whom。
4. **指人先行词**：one, anyone, everyone, none, all 等常用 who。
5. **what 限制**：不能作关系代词，引导名词性从句。
6. **one 作同位语**：泛指的先行词可用 one，后接定语从句。
7. **which 指人**：强调职业、特性、品质或婴儿时可用 which。

### 易错点提醒
1. **介词提前**：牢记 whom/which，避免 that/who。
2. **非限制性从句**：逗号后的定语从句不能用 that。
3. **whose 转换**：指物时 whose 可转换为 of which，但语序可调。
4. **what 误用**：定语从句中不用 what，用 that/which 或 the thing(s) that。
5. **which 指人**：仅限于特定语境（职业、特性、婴儿），一般指人用 who/whom。

## 学习建议
1. **对比记忆**：将只能用 which/who/whom 的情况与只能用 that 的情况对比记忆。
2. **例句积累**：收集 which 指人的例句，理解其适用语境。
3. **错误辨析**：识别 what 误用作关系代词的错误，并改正。
4. **写作应用**：在写作中尝试使用 whose 指物、one 作同位语等高级结构。
5. **真题研究**：分析高考真题中关系代词的特殊用法，巩固规则。
