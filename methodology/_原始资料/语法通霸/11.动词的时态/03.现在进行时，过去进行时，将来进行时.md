# 现在进行时，过去进行时，将来进行时

## 考点1. 现在进行时的概念及谓语构成

**概念：** 表示现阶段或说话时正在进行的动作。

**常用时间状语：** now, at this time, these days, etc.

**谓语构成：** am/is/are + doing

### A. 表示说话时正在进行的动作

- They are playing table tennis now. 他们现在正在打乒乓球。

### B. 表示当前这个时间段或现阶段正在进行的动作（说话时未必在进行）

- Mr. Green is writing another novel.（这一段时间在写，但说话时未必在写）
- Food supplies in the flood-stricken area **are running out**. We must act immediately before there's none left.
- Selecting a mobile phone for personal use is no easy task because technology **is changing** rapidly.

## 考点2. 过去进行时的概念及谓语构成

**概念：** 表示过去某段时间或某一时刻正在发生或进行的行为或动作。

**常用时间状语：** at this time yesterday, at that time。

**谓语构成：** was/were + doing

### A. 表示过去某一时刻正在进行的动作

- — Ouch! You hurt me!
  — I am sorry. But I **didn't mean** any harm. I **was trying** to drive a rat out.
- — What were you doing when Tony phoned you?
  — I had just finished my work and **was starting** to take a shower.
- — Did you catch what I said?
  — Sorry. I **was answering** a text message just now.

### B. 表示过去某个阶段正在进行的行为或动作

- Susan decided not to work on the program at home because she didn't want her parents to know what she **was doing**.
- I called Hannah many times yesterday evening, but I couldn't get through. Her brother **was talking** on the phone all the time!
- — I don't understand why you didn't go to the lecture yesterday afternoon.
  — I'm sorry. But I **was doing** my homework.

## 考点3. 将来进行时的概念及谓语构成

**概念：** 表示将来某一时间正在进行的动作。

**常用时间状语：** soon, this evening, on Sunday, by this time, tomorrow, etc.

**谓语构成：** shall/will + be + doing

### A. 基本用法：表示将来某一时刻或某一阶段正在进行的动作

- Jane can't attend the meeting at 3 o'clock this afternoon because she **will be teaching** a class at that time.
- — Can I call you back at two o'clock this afternoon?
  — I'm sorry, but by then I **will be flying** to Beijing. How about five?
- — Could I use your car tomorrow morning?
  — Sure. I **will be writing** a report at home.

### B. 特殊用法：常表示一种客观上要发生的情况，可以表示计划安排或预测

- — Can you take a message for Mr. Brown?
  — Certainly. I **will be seeing** him about something else in any case, so it **won't be** any bother.
- — Guess what, we've got our visas for a short-term visit to the UK this summer.
  — How nice! You **will be experiencing** a different culture then.

## 考点4. 进行时表示将来

有些动词的现在进行时可以表示按计划、安排将要发生的动作，多用于表示位置转移的动词，如 come, go, leave, start, arrive, fly 等。

- Because the shop **is closing down**, all the T-shirts are sold at half price.
- — It was really very kind of you to give me a lift home.
  — Oh, don't mention it. I **was coming** past your house anyway.
- We **were leaving** very early so we packed the night before.
- James has just arrived, but I didn't know he **was coming** until yesterday.

## 考点5. 进行时表示没有完成

进行时表示动作未完成，而一般过去时表示动作已完成。

- — Has Sam finished his homework today?
  — I have no idea. He **was doing** it this morning.（暗示不一定做完了）
- Shirley **was writing** a book about China last year but I don't know whether she has finished it.（进行时，未完成）
- I **had wanted** to help you but I was not able to spare any time. I **was writing** a paper last night and I'll have to finish it today.

## 考点6. 进行时表示暂时的行为或状态

### A. 用进行时表示暂时的情况

- He is working in Beijing now. 他现在在北京工作。
  （暗含的意思是：他在这之前可能没在北京工作，过一段时间也可能到别的地方工作。现在在北京工作可能是暂时的。）
- I don't really work here, but I **am just helping out** until the new secretary arrives.

### B. be 后跟表示活动、行为类的形容词作表语时

可以用于进行时，表示"短暂的行为或状态"。

- I wasn't sure if he was really interested or if he **was just being** polite.

## 考点7. 进行时和 always 连用，表示"赞赏""反对""埋怨"等情绪

并非强调动作正在进行，而是给经常、反复性的动作添上感情色彩，表示"赞赏""反对"或"埋怨"等各种情绪，用以加强语气。常与进行时连用的此类副词还有 forever, continually, constantly, perpetually 等。

### A. 表示"责备""埋怨""厌烦""恼怒""反对"

- You are always leaving things about. 你总是到处乱丢东西。
- Why are you forever forgetting people's names? 为啥你老是记不住别人的名字？

### B. 表示"赞赏""喜悦""惊讶"等

- She is constantly doing her homework carefully. 她做作业一直都很细心。
- She is forever thinking of others. 她时刻关心他人。
- He is always helping others. 他总是乐于助人。

## 考点8. hope, think, wonder 等用过去进行时使请求的语气更加委婉

- I was wondering if you could do me a favor. 我想请你帮个忙。

## 考点9. 不用进行时的情况（仅供了解）

一些表示事实状态的动词（Non-action Verbs），只说明存在的事实，没有动作的发生，因此不用进行时。

### A. 表示"拥有所属"等事实状态的词

have, belong, possess, exist, include, contain, etc.

### B. 表示心理状态的词

know, believe, understand, think（认为）, suppose, remember, forget, want, need, etc.

### C. 表示状态的系动词

seem, remain, smell, feel, taste, etc.

- **Does** this dictionary **belong** to your brother?
