# 第5讲 还——还原为正常语序

## 概述

*英语中的语序一般是：主语+谓语+宾语+方式状语+地点状语+时间状语。如：He is doing his homework carefully at home now. 但如果语序改变就可能导致句子难以理解。这时我们可以用"还"的办法，把句子还原为正常语序。*

## 详细内容

### 考点1. 动宾之间插入了状语

宾语一般紧跟在动词的后面，但有时由于宾语过长等，常把宾语放到了状语的后面。

- I'd like to thank at this particular moment and upon all of you my dearest lovely mum.

- The professor marched into the lecture hall, placed upon his desk a large jar(罐子) filled with dried beans(豆), and invited the students to guess how many beans the jar contained.【2011全国新课标/完形】

### 考点2. 主谓之间插入了其他成分

- For the President to be silent, I thought was an enormous mistake.【2012美大选第三次辩论】

- The old engineer's eyes still shone bright in the wrinkled(有皱纹的) brown face and his step as he came across the room was steady(稳健), though slow.【2011湖北/单选23】

### 考点3. 在名词和它的后置定语或同位语之间插入了其他成分

有时候，由于修饰成分过长或其他原因，把修饰成分后置，使它与被修饰成分隔开。

- Around this time a lot of stylish(时尚的) but cheaper cars were coming out of Europe, made by companies like BMW(宝马) and Mercedes(奔驰).【高中英语北师大版必修3, P.46】

- Our turn came to meet Mrs. Miller.

- Across the US, stories emerged(浮现) of parents and children reading to each other at night and strangers chatting.

### 考点4. 分裂不定式

在 to 和动词原形之间插入另一个词的不定式，称为**分裂不定式**。

- He liked to half close his eyes.

- To then follow those high-energy rehearsals(排练) with a busy show schedule(日程表) of up to five performances a day, I discovered a new meaning of the words "hard work".【2012北京/完形】

### 考点5. 插入语

插入部分的前后一般都有逗号隔开。在把握句子主要成分时，可以先跳过插入语。

- So, I did and, to my surprise, got some wonderful feedback(反馈).【2012四川/阅读C】

- What, then, is to be done?【2011江苏/任务型阅读】

- Those people, say skeptics(怀疑者), spread(散布) nothing but bad news about the environment.【2011重庆/阅读E】
