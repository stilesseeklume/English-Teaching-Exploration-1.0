# 第3讲 回——往回看以确定是否是后置定语

## 概述

*名词后面可以跟多个修饰成分，修饰成分还可以再跟修饰成分，有时它们交织在一起，难以辨认。当我们遇到辨别不清的成分时，我们可以使用"回"的技巧，即"往回看"，来判断这些成分是否是后置的定语成分。*

## 详细内容

### 考点1. 简短的定语从句

有些定语从句本身都很短，在关系词省略后，就更短，混在句中不容易辨认出来。在断句时，要留意这种情况。在高考的语法题中，关系代词的省略考得比较多，而关系副词的省略却很少涉及。在英语文章中，关系副词的省略也比较常见。

- Cutting down(减少) the number of customers a company loses can make a big difference in its performance.【2011上海/完形】

- The bicycle he rode was not by any means the kind modern cyclists would want to be seen riding on.【2012江西/完形】

- Now when friends and family laugh about the time I slipped during a dance performance, I can laugh too.【2010山东/完形】

### 考点2. 形容词短语作后置定语

形容词短语作定语时常后置，但翻译时要放在前面。

- Why do we ever put ourselves in situations likely to be difficult, embarrassing, and even risky?【2011江苏/任务型阅读】

- Something as simple as setting a goal to have a weekly coffee date with a friend will do.【2011辽宁/阅读】

- On June 11, 2009, nearly 40 of my family and friends gathered in the area close to where I would land while I headed up in the airplane.【2011北京/阅读A】

### 考点3. 多重修饰

一个名词后面可以跟多个修饰成分来修饰这个名词。这些修饰成分可以是一个或多个短语，或一个或多个定语从句，或者是它们的组合。

- For example, there are Chinese shoes made of silk, that were worn by women to tie their feet firmly(紧紧地) to prevent them from growing too much!【2012安徽/阅读A】

- We'll shoot him to death with the Luger(卢格尔手枪) I brought back from Sicily that nobody knows I've got.【英语名著 Catch-22】

- They would be surrounded by a much larger area of green belt(绿化带), also owned by the company, containing not merely farms but also some industrial institutions(产业协会).【2011江西/阅读C】

### 考点4. 连环修饰

有时，名词后面的后置定语又有了自己的后置定语，这就是连环修饰。

- The cat caught the rat that was eating the food that lay on the plate I bought yesterday.

- Police hunting the killer of a part-time police officer stabbed(刺，杀) outside her home in northwest London are seeking a man wearing a hooded top(带有帽兜的上衣) seen running away from the scene.

- A boy was walking home from school when he saw a large, tempting(诱人的) apple on one of the branches of an apple tree hanging out over a tall fence.【2011江苏/完形】

### 考点5. 多重、连环混合修饰

有时在一个句中多重修饰和连环修饰可能同时出现。

- And The People's Archive(人民档案馆) is an online library of the figures(名人) of the city being built up by a digital content company in Cahoots, in which users will be encouraged to add to and comment on the material.【2011四川/阅读C】
