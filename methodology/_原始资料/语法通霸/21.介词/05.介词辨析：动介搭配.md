# 第5讲 介词辨析：动介搭配

## 概述

*本讲辨析常见的动词与介词的搭配，包括 agree with/on/to、arrive at/in、call at/on、die of/from、hear of/from、be made of/from、provide/supply/present 的搭配、result in/from、以及 run/rush/throw at/to 等。*

## 详细内容

### 考点1. agree with, agree on, agree to

#### A. agree with 表示同意某人或某人的意见、想法、分析、解释等
- I don't agree with you. 我不同意你的意见。
- They agreed with this idea. 他们同意这个想法。
- I agree with what you say. 我同意你说的。

#### B. agree with 还表示"食物、天气、工作等适宜某人"
- The weather doesn't agree with me. 这种天气对我不适宜。
- Hard work does not agree with him. 艰苦的工作对他不适宜。

#### C. agree to 主要用来表示一方提出一项建议、安排、计划等，另一方同意协作
- We agreed to their arrangement. 我们同意了他们的安排。
- She agreed to marriage. 她同意结婚。

#### D. agree on/upon 主要指双方通过协商而取得一致意见或达成协议
Both sides agreed on these terms. 双方都同意这些条件。

### 考点2. arrive at 与 arrive in

arrive at + 较小的地点名词，如 school, park, zoo；arrive in + 较大的地点名词，如 Beijing, Shanghai。
- He will arrive at school soon. 他很快就会到学校。
- She has arrived in Nanjing. 她已经到了南京。

### 考点3. call at/on 与 drop in on/at

#### A. call on sb. / call at some place 进行短暂访问
- We called at Mr. Johnson's house yesterday. 我们昨天到约翰逊家访问。
- Does the ship call at Hong Kong? 这艘船在香港停靠吗？
- I'm going to call on one of my former classmates. 我要去看望我的一位老同学。

#### B. drop in on sb. / drop in at some place / drop by 顺便到访
- I dropped in on George on my way home from school. 我在放学回家时顺路拜访了乔治。
- Drop by and pick up that book sometime. 有空了顺便来把那本书拿去。

### 考点4. die of 和 die from

**die of** 主要指疾病、衰老等**内在**的原因；**die from** 重点强调**外部**的原因。
- Bob died of a heart attack, brought on by his lifestyle. 鲍勃死于由他的生活方式引起的突发心脏病。
- Six soldiers are reported to have died from their wounds. 据报道，有6名士兵因伤而亡。

### 考点5. hear of 与 hear from

- **hear of** 听说过（知道某人某事的存在）
- **hear from sb.** 收到某人的来信或电话等

例句：
- Many people have heard of the man's heroic deeds. 许多人都已经听说了这个人的英雄事迹。
- I haven't heard from her for a long time. 我好久没收到她的来信了。

### 考点6. 组成材料 be made of / from / out of

- 用 **be made of** 时成品仍可看出原料
- 用 **be made from** 时成品已看不出原料
- **be made out of** 有时同 be made of 用法相同

例句：
- This box is made of paper. 这个盒子是纸做的。
- Wine is made from grapes. 葡萄酒由葡萄酿成。
- We make bottles (out) of glass. 我们用玻璃制瓶子。
- Make two children's jackets out of this gown. 用这件大褂给孩子们做两件上衣。
- What do you make out of their statements? 你怎么理解他们的陈述？

### 考点7. provide, supply, present 的搭配

在表示"为某人提供某物"时，表达如下：

| 动词 | 搭配方式一 | 搭配方式二 |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **provide** | provide sb. with sth. | provide sth. for sb. |
| **present** | present sb. with sth. | present sth. to sb. |
| **supply** | supply sb. with sth. | supply sth. to sb. |
| **offer** | offer sb. sth. | offer sth. to sb. |

例句：
- The school provided food for the students. 学校为学生提供伙食。
- They provided us with all the books we need. 他们为我们提供所需要的所有书籍。
- Cows supply us with milk. 母牛供给我们牛奶。
- In Britain, milk is supplied to each house in bottles. 在英国，牛奶是装在瓶子里送往每家每户的。
- She offered me a cup of tea. 她给我端了杯茶。
- The little girl presented the princess with a large bouquet of flowers. 小女孩向公主献上了一大束鲜花。

### 考点8. result in 与 result from

- **result from** 由……造成，由……产生
- **result in** 导致

例句：
- The accident resulted in the death of two people. 这场意外事故造成两人死亡。
- His failure resulted from not working hard enough. 他的失败是工作不够努力造成的。

### 考点9. run/rush/throw at/to

用 **at** 时，常含有一定**恶意**；用 **to** 时，只表示**方向**。

| 搭配 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| throw at | 向……投掷（恶意） |
| throw to | 向……扔过去（方向） |
| run at | 向……冲过去（攻击） |
| run to | 向……跑去（方向） |
| rush at | 向……冲过去（攻击） |
| rush to | 向……冲过去（方向） |
| shout at | 对……吼叫（不友好） |
| shout to | 向……喊话（中性） |

例句：
- The bear, wounded badly, gathered all its strength to rush **at** the hunter. 那只熊虽受重伤，仍使出全身力气扑向猎人。
- The troops rushed **to** their destination without a single halt. 部队马不停蹄地赶到了目的地。
- The dog ran **at** the visitor and bit him. 那条狗突然向来客扑去，并咬了他一口。
- He ran **to** the hospital as fast as he could. 他拼命向医院跑去。
- You don't have to shout **at** me. 你没必要朝我嚷嚷。
