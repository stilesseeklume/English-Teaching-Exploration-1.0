# 动词-ing形式

## 概述
动词-ing形式由"动词原形 + -ing"构成，是英语非谓语动词的一种。动词-ing形式可在句中作主语、宾语、表语、定语、宾语补足语和状语，但不能单独作谓语。本页面整合了动词-ing形式的全部用法，涵盖必修阶段的所有相关内容。

## 详细内容

### 1. 动词-ing形式的基本概念
动词-ing形式由动词原形加-ing构成，具有动词和名词的双重特性，但不能单独作谓语。

### 2. 动词-ing形式的句法功能

#### 2.1 作定语
- **单个动词-ing形式**：常放在被修饰词前作前置定语
- **动词-ing形式短语**：常放在被修饰词后作后置定语

**示例**：
- It was great fun walking along the streets, enjoying the relaxing atmosphere!
- I just can't take my eyes off the shining lights on the Christmas trees everywhere.
- Families celebrating the Lunar New Year can enjoy exciting dragon dances and carnivals together.

#### 2.2 作表语
- **表示主语的性质、特征和状态**：作用相当于形容词
- **具体说明主语的内容**：主语等同于表语，两者可互换

**示例**：
- You look amazing tonight.
- Seeing is believing.
- The most important thing is getting there in time.

#### 2.3 作宾语补足语
置于某些及物动词和宾语之后，补充说明宾语的动作或状态。这些及物动词通常是表示感觉和心理状态的动词或使役动词。

**示例**：
- Along the way, we saw many people playing music, singing, and dancing.
- She heard someone knocking at the door.
- You have kept me waiting the whole morning.

#### 2.4 作状语
可以表示时间、条件、原因、让步、结果、方式或伴随状况，其作用相当于状语从句。动词-ing形式的逻辑主语应与句中主语一致。

**示例**：
- Having dressed up nicely, we went out to have some good local food and enjoy the celebrations.
- Thinking of all the people still in need of help, Dr Lin opened a private clinic.
- She sat at the desk reading a newspaper.

#### 2.5 作主语
动词-ing形式可以作主语，表示一个抽象的概念或行为。

**示例**：
- Getting here is quite difficult, so apart from the Sami very few people have ever seen Sarek.
- Being in such a beautiful and wild place makes me feel blessed to be alive.
- Riding on the only steam engine still working in the southeastern United States is a special treat.
- Watching the dolphin and sea lion shows is both educational and fun.

#### 2.6 作宾语
动词-ing形式可以作及物动词的宾语。

**示例**：
- However, you should avoid making this gesture in Brazil and Germany, as it is not considered polite.
- Elsewhere, people favour shaking hands, bowing from the waist, or nodding the head when they meet someone else.
- Experts suggest smiling at yourself in the mirror to make yourself feel happier and stronger.
- If we are feeling down or lonely, there is nothing better than seeing the smiling face of a good friend.

### 3. 动词-ing形式的复习总结
动词-ing形式在句中可充当多种成分，是英语语法中的重要结构。掌握其不同用法对于准确理解和表达英语至关重要。
